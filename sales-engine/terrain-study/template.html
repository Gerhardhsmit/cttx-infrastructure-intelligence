Layer 1 · Carrier

Uplink from {{UPLABEL}}

An uncontended business-grade Vodacom service, delivered by CTTX over a {{UPKM}} km link from {{UPLABEL}} to {{UPTO}}. LTE failover keeps the reserve connected if the carrier path drops.

Layer 2 · Backbone

Two ridge high-sites

{{BACKBONE_TEXT}} Each runs on solar and lithium storage with remote monitoring, so the network stays up through load-shedding.

Layer 3 · Distribution

Every lodge and ops site

Sector radios on each high site reach the lodges, HQ, the anti-poaching base, ecology, the workshop and the gates. A LoRaWAN gateway on the backbone is ready for fence, water-point, pump and fuel sensors.

Operational efficiency

Water points, pumps and fuel tanks report their own status, so there are fewer vehicle trips just to check. Maintenance, ecology and lodges share one communications path.

Infrastructure ownership

High sites, backbone and power belong to the reserve. The network grows with Amakhala instead of depending on each lodge's separate provider.

Executive risk reduction

Management gets live visibility of the network, cameras and sensors, plus a record of operational diligence for owners, insurers and conservation partners.
